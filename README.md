BLACKJACK ❤️♠️♦️♣️                   
Blackjack is a classic casino card game where players compete against the dealer rather than each other. The goal is to build a hand total as close to 21 as possible without exceeding it, using strategy and chance.     

Rules :    
1] You will be given 1000 as your starting amount and you must bet atleast 50             
2] The Deal :::      
        The player receives 2 card face up       
        The dealer receives 1 card face up and 1 card face down        
3} If your initial cards add up to 21 and are a BLACKJACK your payout is in the ratio 3:2           
4] If the dealer initially also has a BLACKJACK your bet will be pushed          
5] Player rules :::          
        After the cards are revealed the player either :        
        Hits - adds a card and increases score. You can hit multiple times until 21 or bust               
        Stand - keep your current hand and end turn         
        Double down - doubles the bet and stand           
        split - If your first two cards have the same rank (e.g., two 8s), you can match your bet to separate them into two independent hands.          
6] Dealer rules :::          
        The dealer reveals their hidden card.            
        The dealer must hit if their total score is 16 or lower.         
        The dealer must stand once their score reaches 17 or higher.           
7] The PAYOUT :::         
        Player Wins (1:1): Your total is closer to 21 than the dealer's, OR the dealer busts while you did not.          
        Natural Blackjack (3:2): Winning with a starting Ace + 10-value card pays 1.5 times your bet.            
        Push (Tie): Your total matches the dealer's total. Your wager is returned to you.       
        Loss: Your total is lower than the dealer's, OR you bust (go over 21), losing your bet immediately.              
8] ENJOY 💸💸💸              

Protip : High Paying High risk

HIGH-LOW ☝️👇

Rules :     
1] The computer will decide a number between 1 to 100     
2] The computer will give a hint by giving a different number      
3] The player now must decide if the decided number by the computer is higher / lower or same as the given number in the hint      
4] The player gets paid accordingly if he wins
